CAPSTONE PROJECT
ASK-COM

AI-Powered E-Consultation System

ASK-COM: AI-Powered E-Consultation System with Availability Tracking and Analytics for the Computer Science Department at Adamson University.

ASK-COM was developed as a centralized consultation platform designed to make student-faculty consultations easier to organize and access.

The system combines consultation scheduling, availability tracking, messaging, analytics, and an AI-driven FAQ chatbot into one platform.

01 Consultation

Students can request and manage consultations with faculty members.

02 Availability Tracking

Helps prevent scheduling conflicts by displaying available consultation periods.

03 AI FAQ Chatbot

Provides quick responses to frequently asked questions.

04 Analytics

Provides useful data and insights about consultation activity.
